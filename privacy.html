Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

This policy covers "YouTube Studio Runner", the internal tool we use to publish videos to our own four YouTube channels: Bonk & Bounce, Why Patch Kids, Kinda Get AI and Humans, I Think.

What the tool accesses

The tool signs in only to our own channels through Google's YouTube API. It uploads our videos, thumbnails and captions, checks the status of our uploads, and reads our own channels' analytics so we can plan better videos.

What we do not do

The tool does not offer sign-in to the public and does not collect, store or share personal data about viewers. We do not sell any data. Viewer data on YouTube stays with YouTube and is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Google API data

Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access tokens stay on our own computer and you can revoke the tool's access at any time from your Google Account permissions.

Children

Bonk & Bounce and Why Patch Kids are set as made for kids on YouTube. We do not collect information from children.

Contact

Reach us through the About page of any of our channels.
